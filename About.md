**Knïps**

A camera app inspired by the analog experience.

**Overview**
Knïps brings back the feeling of analog photography. Instead of taking dozens of photos, it encourages you to capture a single moment with intention.

Photos are not immediately visible after capture. You only see them when you choose to export them or save them to your camera roll—just like waiting for film to be developed.

**Core Idea**
In a world of unlimited digital photos, Knïps focuses on restraint and presence. One photo per moment, not one hundred.

**Key Features**

* Delayed photo reveal for an analog-style experience
* Photos stored locally on your device
* Optional export to camera roll or external services
* Optional date and time stamps rendered directly onto images
* Optional location imprinting
* Optional custom hashtags rendered onto photos

**Printing**
You can send your photos to external printing services via your browser (for example, dm photo printing services). Knïps is not affiliated with these providers.

**Privacy**
Knïps is built with privacy in mind:

* No analytics
* No cloud storage
* No data collection
* Full user control over all photos
